# How NestNote Works

NestNote is a digital babysitter binder—a shared place where families keep household details and sitters see what they need for each job, without digging through old texts and screenshots.

---

## The basics

| Term | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Nest** | Your household's organized stash of info—codes, Wi‑Fi, routines, places, contacts, and more. |
| **Session** | A specific sitting job with dates, times, and the details shared for that booking. |
| **Invite code** | A short code a sitter enters to join a session and see what the family shared. |

Set up your nest once. For each sit, create a session and invite your sitter—they join with the code and see what you included for that booking.

---

## For families (Nest Owner mode)

### 1. Create your nest

From **Menu → Current Nest**, set up your household. Add your address and start filling in the categories that matter—**Household**, **Kids**, **Pets**, **Emergency**, and others.

### 2. Add what sitters need

- **Notes** — details sitters need (alarm codes, allergies, screen-time rules).
- **Routines** — step-by-step checklists (bedtime, meals, handoff).
- **Places** — where things are or where to go (pharmacy, park, spare key).
- **Contacts** — pediatrician, neighbors, grandparents.

You can add photos and organize items into folders so everything stays easy to find.

### 3. Schedule a session

Open **Sessions**, create a session with your dates and times, and choose what from your nest to include. You are not retyping everything each time—you pull from what you already saved.

### 4. Invite your sitter

Send an **invite code** (or invite a saved sitter directly). They enter the code in the app, join the session, and see the information you shared for that sit.

**Tip:** Save sitters you use regularly under **Saved Sitters** so future invites are faster.

---

## For sitters (Sitter mode)

### Join a session

When a family invites you, they share an **invite code**. In NestNote, enter that code to join the session and view the details they included for your booking.

### Session requests

You do not have to wait for the family to start. Create a **session request**, send it to a nest owner, and they receive an invite code to accept or adjust dates and times.

---

## Switching modes

NestNote supports two roles:

- **Nest Owner** — manage your household nest, sessions, and sitters.
- **Sitter** — join sessions, view shared details, and manage your sitting schedule.

Switch modes from the home screen when you need to move between managing your own nest and sitting for others.

---

## Privacy and access

Access is organized around **sessions**. Families choose what to include for each sit—you see what they intend for that booking, not unrelated private notes.

**Nest Members** and **Permanent Access** let trusted family (like grandparents) help manage the nest without sharing everything over text.
