# How Sessions Work

Sessions are how information reaches your sitter. Everything they need for a sit lives on the session—not in scattered texts or screenshots.

---

## Attach what they need

When you create a session, choose what from your nest to include for that booking:

- **Codes & notes** — alarm codes, Wi‑Fi, house rules, allergies
- **Routines** — bedtime, meals, handoff checklists
- **Places** — where things are or where to go
- **Contacts** — pediatrician, neighbors, grandparents

Your sitter only sees what you attach to that session.

---

## Add events for the sit

Need something to happen during the session? Add it as an event on the session calendar—soccer practice, dance class, a pickup time, or anything else on the schedule.

Events keep the day clear for your sitter without extra back-and-forth.
